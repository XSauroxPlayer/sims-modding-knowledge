# TS3 TkMk track mask (0x033260E3)

- RCOL chunk: `'TkMk'`, `version 0x200`, rig key (`QWORD instance, DWORD type, DWORD group`), `FLOAT 1.0`, 28 blank
  bytes, `DWORD count`, then `count` FLOATs in 0.0-1.0 (one weight per rig track/bone).
- Referenced by the Jazz Play node (`TkMk TGI`, see `0x02d5df13.md`).
- Agrees with s3pi `TrackMaskKey` (s3pi-wrappers, https://github.com/ChaosMageX/s3pi-wrappers: version 0x200/0x201, RigKey, VertexAnimBlendWeight, per-track
  weights); the wiki's `FLOAT 1.0` is probably the s3pi `VertexAnimBlendWeight` (inferred from field order), and the wiki does not mention v0x201.
- Use: partial-body animation (e.g. hand grip over a body clip); see `topics/jazz.md` and `topics/ik-and-slots.md`.
