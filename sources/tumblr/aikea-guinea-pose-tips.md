# Pose tips (aikea-guinea, 2013)

Also https://aikea-guinea.tumblr.com/post/62100190711/pose-tips-the-clavicle-theres-a-lot-of-little. Read in a
web browser (tumblr 403 to plain fetch). Most detail is in images (not read).

- **TS2 has no separate bones for the two middle fingers** (TS3 does): when porting TS2 animation, the TS3 middle and
  ring fingers must be driven from one TS2 bone (or posed by hand). Check against the TS2 rig's bone
  list before relying on it.
- Raised or forward/backward arms should start at the **clavicle**: rotating it reduces how far the child bones must
  turn and eases mesh distortion down the arm.
- Index and pinky move most independently; inner fingers move together; mimic the pose with your own hands. Author
  used a modified AMRig with dotted skin to read finger rotation.
