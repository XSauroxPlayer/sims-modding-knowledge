# Short descriptive title (site, section, date)

One or two lines: who wrote it, what it covers, and which topic page uses it (`topics/<name>.md`).

## Key facts
- One fact per bullet, in your own words. Name who said it when the source has several authors.
- At most one short quote per fact, one sentence at most, in quotation marks: "like this".
- Mark claims the source itself flags as untested or a guess.

## Against verified findings
- Where a fact agrees or conflicts with a `verified-in-game` / `verified-in-binary` finding or with another source,
  say so here and link the page. If it conflicts, set `status: contradicted` and add the conflict to the topic
  page's "Contradictions" section.
